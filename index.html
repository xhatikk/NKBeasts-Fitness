EXERCISE LIBRARY

Train every muscle group

Clear exercise selection based on your goal, experience and available equipment.

CHEST

Bench Press

Keep shoulder blades retracted, feet planted and lower the bar under control.

BACK

Lat Pulldown

Pull your elbows toward your sides without swinging your torso.

LEGS

Squat

Brace your core, keep knees tracking over toes and maintain a stable foot.

SHOULDERS

Overhead Press

Keep ribs down and press vertically without overextending the lower back.

BICEPS

Dumbbell Curl

Keep elbows fixed and avoid using momentum to lift the weight.

CORE

Plank

Create a straight line from shoulders to heels and brace the abdomen.
